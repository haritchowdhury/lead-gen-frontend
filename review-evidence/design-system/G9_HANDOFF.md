**Status:** implementation complete; ready for parent review

**Window boundary:** G9 only; G10 nested store-fit/discovery and G11 traffic presentation were not started

## Objective and outcome

The expanded lead now begins with one compact, divider-based overview. Identity and score share the primary region, outreach remains the smaller secondary region, and opening contact evidence recomposes outreach into a full-width ledger. The previous absolute contact overlay, constrained rail, microtype, oversized overlay shadow, and unequal two-column blank regions are removed.

Outcome remains a top-right native `details` badge with a floating evidence surface. Contact evidence remains a native disclosure. Resolved identity stays conditional, and the storefront action is derived only from `resolved_domain`, so an observed product URL cannot become the resolved-storefront target.
